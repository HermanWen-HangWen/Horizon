# Financial news profile (AI-focused)

Use this profile ONLY for financial news items whose primary subject is the
artificial intelligence industry. The defining test: does the item's news
value come primarily from AI economics, or from general economic activity?
Only the former belongs here.

## Route here

- AI company earnings reports, revenue disclosures, profit warnings
- AI lab / AI infrastructure provider funding rounds, IPOs, secondary share
  sales, valuation changes
- AI company mergers and acquisitions (including acquihires of AI teams)
- AI infrastructure capex disclosures (data center buildouts, hyperscaler
  AI spend, AI-focused chipmakers' revenue and capacity updates)
- Major AI industry regulation with material market impact (export controls
  on AI chips, large-scale AI safety / compute legislation, antitrust
  actions against AI companies)
- Government AI investment programs with announced funding amounts
- AI bubble / correction commentary grounded in concrete valuation moves
  (e.g., a named AI company's price action tied to a specific event)

## Do NOT route here

- General macroeconomic data (interest rates, CPI, employment) even if
  markets commentators reference "AI" tangentially
- Non-AI company earnings (retail, banking, energy, consumer goods, even
  traditional big-tech revenue that isn't driven by AI)
- AI product releases, model launches, research results, security incidents,
  open-source releases — those go to the technology news profile
- General stock market commentary, sector rotation essays, market rumors
  without a specific AI company or AI-policy event
- Personal finance or investment advice content, even when mentioning AI
  stocks
- Earnings of AI-adjacent companies where AI is a small or speculative
  share of the story (when in doubt, do not route here)

## Edge cases

- A chipmaker's earnings are AI-related only when AI compute / data center
  demand is the primary driver being reported. A consumer-electronics
  chipmaker's mixed quarter is not.
- A hyperscaler mentioning AI in passing within a broader earnings call
  is not finance-news; only a dedicated AI capex disclosure or AI
  revenue segment is.
- Coverage of "AI in healthcare / finance" verticals belongs to the relevant
  industry profile, not here, unless the item is about the financial
  mechanics of an AI-in-finance company.
